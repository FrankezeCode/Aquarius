## Overview

Aquarius is a **protocol-aware risk intelligence layer** for decentralized finance. It extends Chainlink's horizontal infrastructure — CRE, CCIP, and Confidential Compute — with vertical financial intelligence purpose-built for DeFi position protection.

<Callout type="info" title="Core Principle">
Chainlink executes. Aquarius decides and protects.
</Callout>

Where Chainlink CRE provides generic, protocol-agnostic execution infrastructure, Aquarius provides the **risk interpretation**, **escalation logic**, and **mitigation pathways** that make that execution financially meaningful.

## The Problem

DeFi risk is not generic. Protocols like Aave, Uniswap, and Curve Finance each have:

- **Unique liquidation mechanics** — Aave's health factor model differs fundamentally from Compound's borrowing capacity checks
- **Unique risk thresholds** — A health factor of 1.2 on Aave means something entirely different from an impermanent loss ratio on Uniswap
- **Unique invariant assumptions** — Protocol-specific state transitions that require domain-specific interpretation

Chainlink CRE is protocol-agnostic by design. But risk is protocol-specific. **That gap is Aquarius.**

### What Exists Today

| Layer | Status |
|---|---|
| Price feeds | Chainlink oracles — solved |
| Execution infrastructure | Chainlink CRE — solved |
| Cross-chain messaging | Chainlink CCIP — solved |
| Protocol-specific risk interpretation | **Missing — Aquarius fills this** |

### The Cost of the Gap

Without protocol-aware risk intelligence:

1. **Liquidations happen reactively** — users discover risk too late
2. **Mitigation is manual** — requiring constant monitoring and gas scrambles
3. **Institutional adoption stalls** — TradFi-grade capital requires TradFi-grade protection
4. **Cross-chain risk propagates silently** — a liquidation cascade on Arbitrum has no early warning system for the same position on Ethereum

## How Aquarius Works

Aquarius operates as a three-layer system:

1. **Risk Intelligence Engine** — Continuously monitors protocol state, computes risk metrics, and emits structured risk signals with severity classifications
2. **Aqua Agent** — Executes pre-approved mitigation actions through two pathways: non-custodial (user-approved bounded execution) and vault-backed (pooled capital protection)
3. **Escalation Engine** — Implements an Observe → Warn → Act model that prevents premature execution while ensuring timely response
